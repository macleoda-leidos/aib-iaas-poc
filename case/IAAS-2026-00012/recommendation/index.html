Explanation

Based on a comprehensive analysis of the applicant's financial circumstances, the Debt Arrangement Scheme (DAS) is the most suitable debt solution. Mr Morrison has total unsecured debts of £18,400 across four creditors, with a disposable income of £230 per month after essential expenditure. This disposable income comfortably exceeds the £100/month threshold required for a viable DAS repayment plan.

A DAS would allow Mr Morrison to repay his debts in full over an extended period (approximately 80 months at current disposable income) while retaining his essential vehicle and modest savings. Creditor interest and charges would be frozen from the date of approval, and Mr Morrison would be protected from further creditor action through the statutory moratorium that accompanies an approved DAS.

This recommendation takes into account the applicant's stable employment, absence of prior insolvency proceedings, and the manageable ratio of debt to income. DAS is preferred over PTD as it avoids trustee involvement, protects all assets, and results in full debt repayment — which is achievable given the applicant's circumstances.
