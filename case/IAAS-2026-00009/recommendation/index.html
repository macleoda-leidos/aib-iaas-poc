Explanation

Sequestration (bankruptcy) is recommended as the most appropriate debt solution for Mrs Murray, though with medium confidence due to the complexity introduced by her previous sequestration. Mrs Murray was previously sequestrated in 2015 and discharged in 2018. While a second sequestration is legally permissible in Scotland, it requires careful consideration and typically attracts greater scrutiny from the Accountant in Bankruptcy.

Mrs Murray's current financial position shows total debts of £6,800 with a combined income of £880/month from state pension and benefits. After essential expenditure, her disposable income is £265/month. While this technically supports a DAS arrangement, the presence of a CCJ and the applicant's age and fixed income suggest that a formal insolvency solution providing a clean break would be more appropriate and sustainable.

The Minimal Asset Process (MAP) would normally be the preferred route for someone with Mrs Murray's financial profile, but MAP eligibility may be restricted for applicants with a previous sequestration. This requires manual verification by a qualified adviser. The recommendation is flagged for priority senior adviser review to determine whether MAP or full sequestration is the appropriate route forward.
