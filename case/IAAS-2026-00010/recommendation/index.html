Explanation

A Protected Trust Deed (PTD) is recommended as the most suitable debt solution for Mr Stewart. With total debts of £23,100 and a family home with £35,000 equity, the primary consideration is protecting the property while achieving meaningful debt resolution. A PTD allows the applicant to retain their home provided they maintain agreed contributions over the 4-year trust deed period.

Mr Stewart's disposable income of £240/month would translate to total contributions of approximately £11,520 over 48 months. While this represents less than full repayment of the £23,100 debt, a PTD allows for partial settlement with creditor agreement, typically at 70-80% of the debt value when combined with the trustee's management of non-essential assets.

The confidence level is medium (68%) rather than high due to the applicant's self-employed status. Self-employed income can be variable, and only one year of tax returns has been provided. Additional bank statements have been requested to verify income stability over recent months. If income is confirmed as stable, confidence would increase. The assigned adviser should review the additional documentation before proceeding with a formal PTD recommendation to the applicant.
